Our profit goes to Eat Up

All profit from Market Day goes to Eat Up, an Australian charity that makes free lunches for kids who arrive at school with nothing to eat. Volunteers make the lunches and Eat Up delivers them to schools, because a kid who's hungry finds it hard to focus in class.

  • 40,000+ lunches delivered to schools every week
  • 1,300+ schools supported across Australia
  • 1 in 3 students at those schools need an Eat Up lunch

So whatever you buy, a bracelet or a go at Plinko, it helps put lunch in someone's bag. Figures from eatup.org.au.
